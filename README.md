# Convertible-Wheelchair-to-Bed-Transfer-System
This project proposes the development of a convertible wheelchair-to-bed transfer system that functions both as a conventional wheelchair and as a patient transfer platform.

The wheelchair is designed with a reclining mechanism that allows it to transform into a completely flat surface, effectively acting as a temporary stretcher. 

Once aligned with the hospital bed, the wheelchair can securely dock to the side of the bed using a mechanical locking mechanism to minimize any gap or movement during transfer. The transfer process is facilitated by an integrated low-friction sliding platform and a roller-based mechanism positioned between the wheelchair and the bed. 

Instead of lifting the patient manually, caregivers can gently slide the patient from one surface to the other with minimal physical effort. Adjustable height synchronization further ensures that both surfaces remain level, reducing shear forces and improving patient comfort.
