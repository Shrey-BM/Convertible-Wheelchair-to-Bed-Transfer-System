# Contains detailed explanation (with images and videos) of the following subsystems of the wheel chair:
1. Scissor lift system: To adjust the height/level of the wheel chair.
2. Gas spring system: To recline the wheel chair.
3. Sliding system: Used in the sitting-supine/supine-sitting/sitting-sitting transfers.
4. Rolling system: Used in the sitting-supine/supine-sitting/sitting-sitting transfers.
5. Safety systems: Has a variety of safety mechanisms.
