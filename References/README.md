|---|---|---|
| 1 | [CDC / NIOSH - Safe Patient Handling and Mobility](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/healthcare/sphm/index.html) | Patient handling is the biggest cause of musculoskeletal injuries in healthcare workers. |
| 2 | [CDC / NIOSH - Preventing Back Injuries in Health Care Settings](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/bulletin/2008/lifting.html) | Replacing manual lifting with transfer devices cut patient-transfer injuries by 62%. |
